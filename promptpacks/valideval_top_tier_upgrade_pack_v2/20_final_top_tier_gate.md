# Prompt 20 — Final Top-Tier Gate

## Objective

Decide honestly where the upgraded project belongs.

## Inputs

Read every V2 report.

## Gate categories

- NeurIPS D&B ready candidate.
- COLM/TMLR ready candidate.
- Strong workshop ready.
- Workshop only.
- Not ready.

## Criteria

NeurIPS D&B candidate requires:

- ≥2 real benchmarks or one real benchmark with exceptional external validation,
- artifact-backed ranking/materiality finding,
- external validation or formal blocked-negative result,
- strong related work,
- compiled paper,
- reviewer packet,
- no major unsupported claim,
- reproducible artifacts.

TMLR/COLM candidate requires:

- real panel evidence,
- honest negative/cautionary finding,
- strong artifact,
- polished paper.
