# ValidEval V7.1 Human Resource Accounting

Status: `HUMAN_STUDY_RESOURCE_ACCOUNTING_READY` (planning only).

The planner now distinguishes items per stratum from totals across four strata, unique items from
raw annotation labels, two- versus three-annotator designs, expected adjudication labels, and
benchmark-defect versus pipeline extraction/scoring labels.

The 50-item-per-stratum pilot therefore contains 200 unique items, 400 raw labels with two
annotators or 600 with three, plus 40 expected adjudication labels. The largest listed design has
2,000 unique items, 4,000 or 6,000 raw labels, and 400 expected adjudication labels. Precision,
prevalence, enrichment, agreement, and class-specific precision intervals are reported separately.

No annotators were recruited and no labels were collected. These rows are resource and precision
planning, not human-validation evidence.
