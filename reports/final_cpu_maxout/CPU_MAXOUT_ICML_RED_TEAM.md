## Surviving objections

- Simulator-specific calibration: addressed only within registered generators; external validity remains open.
- Dependence: family and benchmark dependence are explicit, but real panel dependence is unobserved.
- Rank simultaneity: expanded stress found a restricted regime, so scope is narrowed rather than hidden.
- Multiplicity: family aggregates pass, but 21 critical cells fail and prevent a publication-grade label.
- Difficulty confounding: V8 is exploratory and has no licensed item discoveries.
- Repair/transport/human claims: blocked pending real held-out or human evidence.
- Synthetic circularity: known-truth results are supporting method evidence only.
