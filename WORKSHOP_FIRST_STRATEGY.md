# Workshop-First Strategy

## Scope

Focus on ValidEval as a validation harness for benchmark-validity diagnostics:

- claims ledger and evidence states
- synthetic flaw injection
- cross-flaw specificity
- held-out generator transfer
- offline report cards and reviewer-risk checks

## Exclude

- domain packs
- dashboard/badge polish
- GPQA item claims
- unsupported MMLU claims

## Feedback Questions

- Are the diagnostic validation criteria convincing?
- Which external issue-label datasets are most valuable next?
- What thresholds should count as material for benchmark decisions?
