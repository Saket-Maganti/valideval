# Reframed Abstract Draft: Negative External Validation Result

Benchmark scores are often treated as measurements of model ability, but validity is not accuracy: a score can be affected by shortcuts, contamination, scoring artifacts, saturation, low-discrimination items, and other threats to the claimed construct. We present ValidEval, an offline-capable toolkit for auditing benchmark validity as a multidimensional evidence profile rather than a single score. A methodological audit of our legacy controlled synthetic harness found that its current AUCs should be treated only as wiring/sanity-check artifacts because the controlled model behavior is coupled to injected flaw metadata and flaw-specific readout. We therefore center the current evidence on a real external-validation stress test: matrix-derived diagnostics on a 39-model public HELM MMLU panel do not robustly recover structurally aligned MMLU-Redux issue labels. This weak/negative result is cautionary rather than a failure to hide: it shows why benchmark-validity diagnostics need evidence gates before their outputs are converted into benchmark-quality claims. A decoupled flaw-agnostic synthetic harness is scaffolded for future validation, but no new validation run is claimed here.
