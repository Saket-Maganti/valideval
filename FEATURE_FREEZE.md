# Feature Freeze

ValidEval is frozen for NeurIPS evidence work.

## Frozen

- New domain packs.
- Dashboards, badges, certificates, and other presentation features.
- More small Ollama GPQA micro-runs.
- New diagnostics not required by the evidence pivot.
- Embedding deduplication unless it becomes necessary for external validation.

## Allowed

- Importing published per-instance predictions from local files.
- External ground-truth ingestion for MMLU-Redux-style labels.
- Cross-flaw and held-out synthetic validation.
- Panel-validity gates.
- Wide-matrix IRT reporting with explicit proxy limitations.
- Paper, claims, and reviewer-risk scaffolding tied to evidence states.
