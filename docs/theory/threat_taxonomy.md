# Threat Taxonomy

## Shortcut Threats

- Answer label priors
- Lexical clues that bypass reasoning
- Context-independent solution patterns
- Prompt-format artifacts

## Reliability Threats

- Unstable rankings across prompt variants
- High score variance across seeds
- Scoring disagreement
- Sensitivity to irrelevant perturbations

## Item Quality Threats

- Near-zero discrimination
- Negative discrimination
- Saturated items
- Ambiguous or underspecified items

## Contamination Threats

- Exact benchmark text overlap with training corpora
- Near-duplicate benchmark items
- Memorization-like performance patterns

## Coverage Threats

- Missing construct facets
- Overrepresented item formats
- Redundant content clusters
- Weak or missing construct metadata

## Criterion And Consequential Threats

- Low correlation with external outcomes
- Optimization against a proxy that damages the target construct
- Ranking shifts that encourage benchmark-specific training artifacts
