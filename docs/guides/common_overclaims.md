# Common Overclaims

Avoid these claims:

- "This diagnostic proves the benchmark is invalid."
- "No local overlap means the benchmark is clean."
- "The repaired subset is the true benchmark."
- "The validity-adjusted ranking is the true ranking."
- "Human labels are perfect ground truth."
- "A certificate level is a total benchmark score."

Safer phrasing:

- "Evidence is consistent with a possible validity threat under this diagnostic."
- "No local evidence was found in the searched corpus."
- "This repaired subset is advisory and requires author review."
- "This ranking view is conditional on the scorer, model panel, and diagnostics run."
- "Human validation provides protocol evidence and may require adjudication."
